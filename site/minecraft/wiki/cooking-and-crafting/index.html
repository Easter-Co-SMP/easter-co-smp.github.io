Hunger, healing and effects

Cooking and baking are useful preparations, not just hunger management. Natural regeneration is disabled, and custom dishes can provide healing or effects while restoring no hunger. Keep an ordinary hunger-restoring staple alongside your prepared supplies.

The restored staples include apples, bread, common cooked meats and berries. Their normal nutrition is preserved while the pack can add healing behavior. A renamed or remodeled item may be a custom ingredient: use its displayed name and recipe rather than its underlying vanilla item type.

The recipe book

The pack adds recipes through ordinary crafting, furnace cooking, stonecutting, smithing and other existing station types. New ingredients and outputs can unlock related recipes as you obtain them; the recipe book is useful, but it is not a promise that every recipe is visible before its conditions are met.

  1. Read the item’s displayed name and effects when you obtain an unfamiliar ingredient.
  2. Check the recipe book at the station relevant to that ingredient. Furnace recipes still need fuel.
  3. Check the output before committing valuable supplies. Custom item appearance and purpose can differ from the vanilla item used to represent it.

Useful first recipes

These three furnace recipes use readily recognizable ingredients. Each produces one cooked item and is a custom healing/effect supply rather than a hunger replacement.

Carry enough for the journey rather than relying on those short buffs as permanent protection. The healing burst is not permission to ignore damage, and Grilled Melon’s fire resistance is much shorter than a long exploration trip.

Prepare for the journey

Choose supplies by their job: hunger, immediate recovery, a defensive effect or equipment maintenance. Fancy dishes are not automatically better for every job, and a zero-nutrition healing dish will not solve starvation.

  • Cooking: check cooked outputs before using a familiar vanilla ingredient in bulk.
  • Baking and other recipes: keep an ingredient when obtaining it reveals a useful follow-on recipe.
  • Crafting and smithing: read the output’s changed stats and effects; the equipment guide covers useful upgrade differences.
