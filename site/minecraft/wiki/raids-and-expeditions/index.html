Before you set out

Prepare for sustained damage and a longer fight. A full hunger bar will not supply natural healing, and ordinary death still leaves your backpack at the death site.

  • Bring both hunger food and healing/effect supplies; short food buffs are not a substitute for long-duration protection.
  • Keep recovery essentials in protected equipment or hotbar slots, while remembering the separate bounty-death rules.
  • Agree on supply and recovery plans with companions. Team size is practical advice, not a fixed class or party-entry requirement.

Stronger Nether creatures

The custom Nether pass gives selected creatures doubled baseline health and increases supported melee attack attributes. Its affected types include blazes, wither skeletons, piglins, piglin brutes, hoglins, zoglins, ghasts, magma cubes, endermen and striders.

Melee values for the supported hostile types are increased by 50%. Ghast projectiles and attacks without that melee attribute are not made stronger merely by this rule. Do not assume a familiar-looking enemy will fall in the same number of hits.

Navy jurisdiction does not extend into the Nether, but an existing bounty can still be claimed there. Crossing dimensions is not a bounty reset.

The dragon encounter

The dragon’s custom maximum health is 1,600 health points, or 800 hearts. This tuning increases its health pool; it does not add a new attack-damage multiplier through the same rule.

Plan for endurance: supplies, recovery and coordination matter more when you cannot count on hunger-based healing. Taking companions is a recommendation for a demanding fight, not an enforced minimum party size.

A dragon summoned through the normal repeat-encounter path receives the health tuning too. This guide covers the custom change rather than repeating every vanilla phase and crystal mechanic.

Rewards and repeat visits

Completing the pack’s dragon-kill advancement triggers a special item drop at the centre of the End. The reward follows advancement eligibility; it is not an automatic personal payout for everyone present.

Repeat attempts use the encounter’s normal access and respawn conditions, with the custom health tuning applied again. A repeat attempt does not automatically grant that advancement reward again.

Recover before committing another attempt. Check current health, remaining supplies and any outstanding bounty rather than assuming the next trip starts with a clean slate.
