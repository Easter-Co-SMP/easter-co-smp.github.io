Crews & property

Use mutual crew alliances for trusted access to homes and named ships, while keeping ownership, theft and house-editing permissions separate.

How a crew alliance works

Your crew is the set of players with whom you have accepted a mutual alliance. It is not a captain-owned guild or a transitive friend network. If you ally with River, and River allies with Ash, you and Ash are still strangers unless you also accept an alliance.

The same alliance applies to homes and named ships. The owner does not have to stay online for a crew member to use the alliance. Each pair controls its own relationship; someone cannot recruit another person into your crew on your behalf.

Adding and removing an ally

Use the exact player name in place of River. Sending a request alone grants no alliance: the other person must accept.

Requests persist when someone is offline. Use the request list after returning; do not assume an unseen chat prompt means no request exists.

Player crew commands
CommandResult
/crew add RiverAsk River to ally with you; an online target receives a chat prompt.
/crew accept RiverAccept a request River sent to you.
/crew decline RiverDecline River’s request.
/crew remove RiverEnd your alliance or clear a request between you.
/crew requestsList pending requests.
/crew listList your mutual allies.

Access is not ownership

Crew can enter and leave an ally’s registered home, use its containers and workstations, and pilot the ally’s named ship. They can take or deposit cargo without the stranger-theft offence.

Crew status does not turn friendly violence into a blanket exemption: murdering a non-pirate ally still has a separate law consequence.

Important property boundaries
ActionRule
Open a registered home’s entranceOwner and crew may enter; someone already inside can leave.
Take from an owned home or named shipOwner and crew are permitted; strangers can incur theft in Navy jurisdiction. A reachable chest is not physically locked by this rule.
Place or break furniture in a homeOwner only, within the measured interior and allowed furnishing types.
Alter walls, roof, doorway or gardenNot a resident permission, even for the owner.

Living in a registered house

Registered player houses preserve their structure and allow their owner to furnish the interior. The property system recognizes the assigned owner; entering a house or seeing a For Sale sign does not claim it.

There is no player-facing purchase command to follow from a For Sale sign. Arrange a house assignment with the server’s staff, then use crew alliances for access. Do not use operator registration commands as a player purchase flow.

Removing an ally revokes the alliance permission. It does not retrieve items they already took or undo changes already made. For a ship, claiming another vessel also changes your ownership; read that guide before transferring ships.
