The world and its regions

Read region notices, distinguish protection from Navy jurisdiction, and use the world chart without mistaking a saved snapshot for live conditions.

Read the region notice

Crossing a boundary prints the area you left and entered, followed by its protection labels. The area name is blue; read the words as well as the colours.

The three protection labels
LabelMeaning
PVP: Safe / AllowedOrdinary combat between non-pirate players is protected / allowed. Pirate involvement and explosions are exceptions.
PVE: Safe / DangerousThe region removes the hostile mobs covered by its protection / does not apply that removal. It does not prevent falls, drowning or every source of harm.
Mining: Illegal / LegalGeneral block edits are protected / not blocked by that regional rule. Placement and world-editing items also matter; houses have separate restrictions.

Protection and Navy territory

Navy-controlled territory determines where the Crown records offences and where its guards enforce the law. PVP, PVE and edit protection are independent settings: do not infer one from another.

Navy jurisdiction applies only in the Overworld. Outside named areas, the notice calls the location The Wilds followed by its biome. A bounty already on your head remains claimable beyond Navy territory.

PVP protection checks both players’ positions, so attacking across its edge does not bypass the non-pirate protection. If either player is a pirate, the protection does not shelter the fight. Explosions also retain their separate damage rules.

Places and regions

Use the starting island as a reference point for preparing a journey, finding services and reading the current area rules. Public region names and borders can change as the world develops; the chart is a better directory than a copied coordinate list.

  • Services: use the named service menus for banking, supplies, healing and law-related actions.
  • Homes: an owned house has its own entry and furnishing rules, even when the surrounding region permits edits.
  • Harbours and water: check Navy jurisdiction before attacking patrols or taking a protected owner’s ship.

The world chart

The public chart shows saved terrain, regions and non-player markers. It does not show player locations. Ship and NPC positions can be older than the terrain or your current session; a marker is not proof something is still there.

Use layer toggles to reduce clutter. Personal drawings attach to world coordinates and are stored on your device. Check the in-game entry notice when a legal or protection boundary matters immediately.
