Money & banking

Sterling is split between your spendable wallet and bank balance. Learn deposits, withdrawals, obol exchange, and why a bank balance cannot pay automatically.

Your two balances

The wallet pays for custom purchases and charges and can hold up to £100.00. The bank is a separate deposit balance with no gameplay deposit ceiling. Banked money must be withdrawn before spending and is not automatically used to settle a bounty.

A new account starts with £50.00 in the wallet and £50.00 in the bank. Returning, spending down to zero or resetting does not grant the starting money again. Your bounty is the amount on your head; debt means a negative wallet. Neither is another spendable account.

Outstanding bounty blocks bank transactions. Settle the law issue before relying on a withdrawal to fund your next purchase.

Deposits and withdrawals

Open Jim’s or Bob’s service menu by interacting with a banker, or use /villager menu jim or /villager menu bob. These are player service commands, so you can use the menu without hunting for a physical NPC.

Depositing £1.25 from a £10.00 wallet leaves £8.75 in the wallet and adds £1.25 to the bank. A deposit does not clear a bounty or erase debt.

  1. Check Wallet and Banked in the menu.
  2. Enter pounds and pence, such as 1.25, for a sterling transaction.
  3. Choose Deposit to move money to the bank, or Withdraw to move it back.
  4. Read the result before making another transfer. The requested amount must fit the source balance and the wallet’s capacity.

Exchanging obols

Obols are physical items. Four obols are worth £0.01. Buy Obols spends wallet money for the coins; Sell Obols takes coins and credits the wallet.

The obol counter accepts positive multiples of four. Five carried obols let you sell four and keep one. Buying needs inventory space and enough wallet money; selling needs the items and room below the wallet cap. A failed exchange is refused rather than partially filled.

The input unit changes with the button

Deposit and Withdraw take pounds. Buy Obols and Sell Obols take a count of obols. Enter 8 to exchange eight obols for £0.02, not to exchange £8.00.

Payments and debt

A large bank balance does not let an empty wallet buy from a custom shop. Prices on shop buttons apply to the displayed quantity; make inventory space first, because purchased items that do not fit can fall on the ground.

Ordinary death does not take sterling, but a bounty claim can debit more than your wallet holds. Jail handles that negative balance. Banked money remains separate, and the reset command does not zero either account.
