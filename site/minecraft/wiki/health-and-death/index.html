Health & death

Learn how to heal without natural regeneration, what ordinary death keeps, and why bounty deaths and a deliberate reset need extra care.

Healing and hunger

Natural health regeneration is disabled: filling hunger does not slowly heal you. The usual hunger-restoring staples still feed you, and many also carry custom healing effects. Prepared dishes often provide healing or buffs without restoring hunger at all.

Carry a hunger supply and a healing supply. Read the displayed effects of unfamiliar foods; a useful healing dish can still leave you hungry. Zuri’s Mend service restores current health to your current maximum, with a five-minute game-time cooldown. It does not restore lost maximum hearts.

What drops on ordinary death

Ordinary death drops the 27 backpack slots above the hotbar. It keeps the hotbar, equipped armour, offhand and XP. Ender-chest contents are not part of the carried backpack.

  • Put the equipment you most need for recovery in the hotbar or equipped slots.
  • Dropped backpack items remain items in the world; do not treat their recovery as guaranteed.
  • A claimed pirate bounty or Navy arrest strips carried equipment too. Read Law & bounties before relying on ordinary death protection.

Maximum health changes

Death reduces maximum health by one heart on Easy/Normal and two hearts on Hard. A lower limit prevents endless loss, but the limit is separate from your current health.

On Easy the floor is four hearts. Outside Easy, your personal floor governs the limit; progression can lower that floor to two hearts. The maximum ceiling is 30 hearts. Crystal Hearts add one maximum heart when successfully used, subject to the ceiling and their use conditions. Recovering ordinary health does not undo a maximum-heart penalty.

Count hearts, not health points

Two health points make one heart. A four-point floor is two visible hearts, not four.

A deliberate reset drops everything

The player command /trigger reset is an emergency return to the starting island, with a 30-second game-time cooldown. It kills your character and drops everything carried, including armour, hotbar and offhand. XP is restored and wallet/bank balances are left alone.

Bounty consequences and confinement still have their own rules. Do not use suicide or reset as a plan for clearing a bounty or leaving jail.

Reset is a loss-causing action

It is not a free teleport, does not grant another starter kit, and does not erase wallet debt. Ordinary death’s equipment protection does not apply.
