Open a service menu

The named service characters use conversation menus rather than ordinary villager trading. Interact with a service NPC, or use the player command /villager menu followed by its service ID. The command opens the same menu without requiring that character to be standing nearby.

Menu access does not bypass the transaction’s eligibility checks. Outstanding bounty blocks custom shops and bank transactions. Prisoners cannot use outside services. Read the result in chat after an action.

Ordinary named islander villagers are different: they retain their normal villager trade screen. A service character and a regular villager are not interchangeable.

Shops and supplies

Use the menu’s current prices and quantities instead of treating this guide as a stock list. Custom shops take wallet sterling; make inventory space before buying.

Banking, healing and law

Use these service menus for their specific actions. The bank, bounty and jail rules determine whether an action is available.

Zuri’s Mend is free and restores current health on a five-minute game-time cooldown. Bless costs £0.01 and grants Regeneration II for 30 seconds plus Absorption II for two minutes. It is a paid service, so shop eligibility still matters.

When a service refuses

A menu can open even when its action cannot be completed. Check your wallet, bounty and any cooldown first. A banker needs the correct input unit; a prisoner-payment helper and bounty poster must be clean Navy with no bounty and no active Criminal mark.

The Harbourmaster’s conversation is not a working fast-travel purchase. Do not plan a return journey around buying passage from that dialogue.

Use the service commands above if a physical NPC is absent. A map marker or the existence of a name in a guide does not guarantee a character is currently at that location.
