Law & bounties

Know where Navy law applies, how offences change your status, who can claim a bounty, and how payment or jail settles the consequences.

Where Navy law applies

New offences are recorded only in Navy-controlled Overworld regions. There is no Navy jurisdiction in the Nether, End or uncontrolled Wilds. Existing bounties remain on the ledger, and eligible players can claim them outside Navy territory and in other dimensions.

Ordinary Navy enforcement stays within its jurisdiction. A PVP-safe region is a separate protection rule: it does not shelter a fight involving a pirate or suppress explosion damage. Inside Navy-controlled areas, explosions preserve natural terrain while recorded player-placed blocks can break. Blasts still damage exposed players and creatures.

Status and offences

You begin in the Navy band. A bounty below £0.05 remains in that band; £0.05–£0.99 is the citizen band, and £1.00 or more makes you a pirate. A Criminal mark is a separate timer, not a wallet balance or a selectable class.

Any outstanding bounty blocks custom shops and banking. Offences start or renew the Criminal timer from 15 minutes, with 15 seconds added for each repeat offence after the first. The mark counts down while you are online. Some ordinary hit offences escalate with a recent streak and are tripled while marked; flat charges are not multiplied.

Violence against protected players, villagers, traders or Navy personnel, named-animal harm, theft and unauthorized ship seizure also have consequences. Killing an ally who is not a pirate carries triple the ordinary £1.25 player-murder charge; an alliance does not legalize murder.

Claims, payment and jail

Pirates cannot collect killing-blow or assist bounty payouts. Eligible player kills can claim a pirate bounty anywhere. Combat tags last five minutes; an eligible recent attacker can receive a claim when the target dies to the environment. An unclaimed suicide does not clear piracy.

A claimed bounty debits the target’s wallet, strips carried equipment and settles the pirate status. A negative wallet sends the respawning player to jail. A Navy arrest in its territory instead confiscates pocket money and turns the full bounty, plus prior wallet debt, into a sentence. Banked money is not automatically taken.

Jail repays £0.15 per minute of logged-in time in the cell at normal game speed, releasing you when the wallet debt reaches zero. Logging out does not serve time. Prisoners cannot use outside services and have prison-only chat. A clean Navy player with no bounty or Criminal mark can pay a prisoner’s remaining debt through Silas.

Below £1.00, a guard’s payment option can settle a bounty from your wallet. Zuri can forgive up to £0.05 once the Criminal mark has faded. Neither is a prisoner’s way to bypass a sentence.

Service and posted bounties

A qualifying Navy player earns £0.05 directly into the bank for a complete, uninterrupted daytime on in-game week days 1–6. Day 7 does not pay. Stay online, alive, out of prison and in the Navy band throughout daytime; joining late, dying, disconnecting, losing eligibility or a server restart invalidates that daytime.

The Overworld clock governs this reward even while you visit another dimension. Payment is made at dusk, once for that world day.

A clean Navy player can fund an increase to an already-pirate player’s bounty through Pike. The Crown takes one eighth, rounded down to whole pennies: posting £10.00 adds £8.75 to the target and pays £1.25 tax. This is funded from the payer’s wallet; it is not a way to make a lawful player a pirate.
