What is different here

Easter Co. SMP changes several familiar survival rules. Prepare around these differences before leaving the starting area.

  • A full hunger bar does not provide natural health regeneration. Carry food for hunger and a way to heal.
  • Ordinary death keeps your hotbar, worn armour and offhand, but drops the 27-slot backpack. Bounty deaths and a deliberate reset have harsher rules.
  • Named regions have separate combat, hostile-mob and building protections. Navy jurisdiction is another rule; a protected area is not immunity from everything.
  • Custom shops spend sterling from your wallet. Money in the bank must be withdrawn before spending.
  • Ships, crew alliances, named-animal law and equipment upgrades have custom rules. The guides below cover the parts that differ from vanilla.

Your first steps

The launcher connects you directly to the server. If you return to the main menu, choose Play Now to reconnect; Options and Quit remain available.

  1. Read the area notice in chat. Check its PVP, PVE and Mining labels before fighting or editing the surroundings.
  2. Check your inventory and balances. A first-time account receives £50.00 in the wallet and £50.00 in the bank; a completely empty first-time inventory also receives leather armour and wooden starter tools.
  3. Keep essentials in the hotbar and arrange food plus healing before travelling. Check each dish’s effect instead of assuming it fills hunger.
  4. Use Village services for shop and banker menus, then choose a destination on the world map.

Find your way

The world chart shows named areas and public ship, patrol, villager and mob markers. It is a saved snapshot, so check in-game notices for the rules at your current location. Your own map drawings stay on your device.

Choose your next goal

There is no class-selection screen to complete before playing. Navy Conscript, Pirate Raider and Bounty Hunter describe ways to play; the actual law and status rules determine which services and protections apply.

  • Sail: learn the helm controls and ownership consequences before claiming a vessel.
  • Settle: use a crew alliance for trusted access, while keeping house-edit restrictions in mind.
  • Explore: prepare healing and equipment before testing the strengthened Nether creatures or dragon.
