Ships & sailing

Learn custom piloting and reverse rowing, claim or capture a vessel, and understand what renaming, abandoning or destroying a ship changes.

Before you sail

At the helm of a sailing ship, the forward and backward movement controls open and close the sail. Steering follows a turning arc rather than rotating a large hull freely on the spot. The default forward/backward bindings are W and S.

Reverse rowing is for freeing and positioning the ship, not a second sailing throttle. These controls describe sailing hulls; do not expect the reverse-rowing rule on an ordinary paddle boat.

  1. Raise the sail with forward input and leave room to turn: larger hulls need a wider arc.
  2. To reverse-row, lower the sail and let forward motion stop, then keep holding backward input. The ship moves slowly astern while the input is held.
  3. Release backward input to stop the rowing stroke. If a bank or obstacle prevents travel, steering does not grant a free turn.

Claiming and naming a ship

You can own and name one registered ship at a time. Take the helm and open that ship’s cargo inventory to see the pilot’s controls. On an unowned or other-owned vessel, Claim/Capture becomes available when its defending crew is gone.

Taking a protected owner’s ship or a Navy patrol ship can be piracy in Navy jurisdiction. A pirate owner’s hull does not receive that Crown protection.

  1. Defeat the defending crew where capture is required, then secure the helm.
  2. Choose Claim/Capture in the cargo controls.
  3. Choose Rename Ship, enter a name of 1–48 characters, and use Save name or Enter.
Claiming replaces your previous registration

Acquiring another ship releases your previous ownership and clears the old ship’s name. It does not give you a second registered vessel.

Cargo and crew access

A crew alliance lets an ally pilot your named ship without the owner being online. It also makes taking from the hold lawful between those allies. The alliance is with you, not every friend of every crew member.

A cargo hold is not a magically locked chest. Someone who can reach it may be able to move items; unauthorized withdrawal is judged as theft where Navy law applies. Depositing or rearranging items is not the same as taking them.

Abandoning and scuttling

Abandon Ship removes your ownership and the ship’s name. It does not sink the hull. The Scuttle action is offered for a vessel you do not own and asks for a second confirmation; it destroys the vessel through its normal destruction behavior.

Automatic cleanup applies only when a vessel has no name, owner, inventory or pilot and has been unpiloted for more than one in-game day. An empty-looking ship with stored cargo or an owner does not meet all of those conditions.

Recover cargo before destroying a vessel

Scuttling is destructive. Cargo follows the ship’s normal destruction/drop behavior, so destruction is not a safe cargo-transfer method.

Chart a course

Use the chart to plan around coastlines and regions, then confirm the local notice in game. Patrol and ship markers are last observed positions, not live tracking.
